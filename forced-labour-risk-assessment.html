Overview

Forced labour risk can be difficult for organisations to identify across complex, global supplier networks. The challenge is not only understanding risk—it is gathering the right information in a consistent way and turning it into a process that teams can act on.

This case study uses public product information and simplified descriptions of my contribution. It does not include production screens, customer data, or proprietary methodology.

My role

I designed the core Maxsight experience for Moody's Forced Labor Risk Assessment, including the complete supplier-survey flow and its interactions. The work helped establish a new, customisable survey capability within the platform, built specifically to support this type of due-diligence workflow.

The challenge

Businesses need to assess forced-labour exposure across supplier networks that can span different countries, sectors, and operating models. The product needed to help teams create supplier assessments within Maxsight, gather structured information directly from suppliers, make a complex survey feel manageable to complete, and turn responses into inputs for a wider risk assessment.

A generic form or survey tool would not provide the flexibility or product integration required, so the team created a bespoke survey capability from the ground up.

Designing the journey

I structured the experience around a clear journey: create supplier profile → send survey → collect responses → assess risk → benchmark results → identify mitigation actions.

This created a shared understanding of the workflow across the product team, while ensuring individual users could focus on the task in front of them.

A bespoke survey capability

The survey experience had to balance two competing needs: gathering detailed, structured information and keeping completion clear and approachable.

I designed the interaction model from scratch—question flow, progress states, and reusable interface patterns—so the patterns could support customisation while remaining consistent within the Maxsight product experience.

From information to action

The assessment evaluates supplier risk across country, industry, and business-practice factors. My design work focused on making the data-collection stage understandable and purposeful, so the information gathered through the survey could contribute to a meaningful wider risk view.

The goal was not simply to collect more data. It was to support a workflow that helps organisations understand where attention and mitigation may be needed.

Outcome

The Forced Labor Risk Assessment launched in 2025 through a collaboration between Moody's and the University of Nottingham's Rights Lab. The public solution combines Rights Lab research and scoring methods with Moody's technology to help organisations assess supply-chain exposure, collect supplier information, prioritise risk, and support mitigation activity.

My contribution established the core digital assessment experience and the custom survey workflow that enabled this research-led methodology to function as a usable product within Maxsight.
